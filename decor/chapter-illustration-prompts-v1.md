# Chapter illustration prompt set v1

Built-in image generation. Created 2026-09-04.

Style reference: `hero-comic-studio-v2.jpg`

## Shared prompt

Use case: illustration-story

Asset type: square chapter vignette for a Japanese story-manga creation questionnaire.

Create a completely new square illustration matching the style reference's sophisticated contemporary Japanese editorial manga: expressive charcoal ink line, halftone dots, warm off-white paper texture, and sparse muted teal, terracotta and mustard watercolor accents. Make the composition clear at 120–160px and leave comfortable breathing room.

Constraints: no readable text, letters or numbers; no logos; no watermark; no flowers, ribbons, hearts, gender stereotypes, corporate clip art, beauty-ad styling, or glossy anime finish; no captions or dialogue.

## Chapter 1 — 届けたい相手とゴール

Visualize who this story should reach and what future it should create. Show an androgynous adult protagonist in three-quarter back view beside empty floating comic panels. In a farther panel, a small diverse audience looks back with interest. Connect them with a path of halftone dots. Mood: hopeful, thoughtful, the first page of a personal story.

## Chapter 2 — あなたとお仕事

Visualize the value of the person's work. Show a coherent three-panel montage: an adult professional listening across a small table, hands arranging practical work tools and blank notes, and a customer leaving with a relieved posture. Use varied, gender-balanced adult silhouettes without choosing a specific profession. Mood: capable, trustworthy, quietly rewarding.

## Chapter 3 — あなたの物語

Visualize a personal turning point. Show the same adult across three diagonal comic panels: sitting alone in a dim cramped scene, pausing at a doorway as light enters, then stepping into a bright open scene with a calm hopeful expression. Make the emotional transition immediate. Mood: honest struggle turning into possibility, without melodrama.

## Chapter 4 — 入れたいこと・入れたくないこと

Visualize choosing what belongs in the story and protecting what stays private. Show two pairs of adult hands editing blank storyboard panels: one meaningful panel circled with a colored pencil, one sensitive panel covered by a clean protective sheet, and an eraser nearby. Mood: trust, care, collaborative editing. All paper and panels remain blank.

## Chapter 5 — 見た目・絵柄

Visualize the person becoming a manga character. Show an artist's character design sheet: one gender-neutral adult in front and side view plus four expressive face studies—calm, joy, surprise and determination. Add a brush pen and three color swatches. Clothing is simple and gender-neutral. Mood: discovery, anticipation, personal but professional.

## Chapter 6 — ほかの登場人物

Visualize people becoming characters in the story. Show four distinct adults in overlapping comic panels: a main protagonist in the center and three supporting people at different distances, with varied ages, gender presentation, hairstyles and clothing. Eye lines and gestures suggest relationships, support and shared history rather than a posed photo. Mood: connection, memory, human warmth.

## Chapter 7 — 公開と仕上げ

Visualize the finished story reaching people. Show a hand holding a smartphone with a vertical carousel of three finished-looking but completely blank comic panels. Around it, diverse adult readers react with recognition, warmth and interest. A subtle paper-plane motion line moves out from the phone. Mood: completion, pride, connection. No vanity metrics; all phone panels remain blank.
